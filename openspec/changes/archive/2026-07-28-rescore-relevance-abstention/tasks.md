Record which grid points fail and why.

  **Floor axis** (grid step 0.05). Admissible on D8.2 (`overAbstentionRate = 0` at both `k`): `null`, 0.20, 0.25, 0.30. Failing: **0.35 → 0.063**, **0.40 → 0.250**, **0.45–0.60 → 0.438–0.625**.   The grid explains why, and the sweep prints the verdict: the two level distributions **overlap**. Lowest gold-bearing leader level 0.333 (`q-nimbus-deploy-pipeline-latest`), highest abstention leader level 0.455 (`q-abstain-global-changelog`), with `q-abstain-nimbus-tracing-vendor` at 0.375 also inside the overlap. **No floor abstains on every empty-gold query without rejecting a gold-bearing one.** The widest admissible window is `(0.307, 0.333]` — 0.026 wide, bounded below by one individual abstention query's score and above by one individual gold query's score.   **Ratio axis** (grid step 0.1, at floor `null`).   **The confound this originally exposed has since been removed at the source, and that changes the standing of this measurement.** As first implemented, enabling the ratio activated **two** mechanisms: the filter, and a narrowing of the pool `applyRankingBoost` sorts from the rank window (64 at the default page) to the page-sized gate window (16). `ratio = 0.00` was added to isolate the second — it keeps every row the filter sees — and came out byte-identical to `null`, but that control was weak because only 2 of 24 queries had a pool larger than the window, so 22 of them could not observe the narrowing at all.

  The review that followed showed the page-sized window was itself a defect (`design.md` D4: it made both gates a function of the requested `offset` and of fusion order), and the gate now levels the **whole fused pool**. The second mechanism therefore no longer exists: rows leave the pool only because the filter cut them. `ratio = 0.00` is byte-identical to `null` again on the regenerated grid, and it is now a _strong_ control — every one of the 24 queries observes the filter, because the filter always sees the entire pool. **This measurement isolates one mechanism, which is what it could not do before.**

  Re-measured on the pool-wide gate, 0.40 still clears every criterion, still with two full steps of admissible band on each side (0.00–0.60 all pass; R@8 0.969 at 0.50 and 0.60 is above the 0.95 committed floor).
